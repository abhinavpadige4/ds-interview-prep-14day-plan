## Success Tips

1. **Consistency:** Stick to the daily schedule as much as possible
2. **Active Learning:** Type out code rather than copying/pasting
3. **Teach Others:** Explain concepts to reinforce your understanding
4. **Build Projects:** Apply concepts to small personal projects
5. **Review Regularly:** Use spaced repetition for key concepts
6. **Stay Healthy:** Take breaks, exercise, and maintain work-life balance
